# MoveAI Product Strategy

## Mission

Empower anyone to master human movement by translating elite-coach expertise into real-time, personalized feedback — helping users train smarter, safer, and with measurable progress.

## Vision

Bring elite-coach feedback to everyone — instant, visual, and powered by AI.

## Why MoveAI Wins

The global fitness ecosystem is saturated with tracking and content tools but lacks actionable feedback on execution quality. MoveAI closes that gap by merging:

* **Computer-Vision Precision:** Uses Apple Vision / CoreML models for per-frame biomechanical analysis.  
* **Contextual Coaching Layer:** Converts pose data into cues, safety warnings, and personalized improvement paths.  
* **Habit Reinforcement:** Tracks consistency and gamifies long-term mastery with streaks, leaderboards, and progress dashboards.

Unlike generic workout apps, MoveAI doesn't just log what you did — it teaches you how to move better.

## North Star Metric

*% of active users who show ≥ 30 % Form Improvement Index within 4 weeks.*

This single metric unites user value (skill improvement), product value (model accuracy), and business value (retention).

## 2026 Strategic Themes

1. **Core Feedback Loop:** prove that AI feedback measurably improves form.  
2. **Safety & Consistency:** expand to multi-rep tracking, risk detection, and data-driven cues.  
3. **Coaching Intelligence:** evolve into a personalized training companion with predictive insights and progress analytics.

## Success Framework

MoveAI tracks impact across the HEART framework:

* **Happiness:** Feedback Satisfaction ≥ 4.3 / 5  
* **Engagement:** ≥ 3 form analyses / user / week  
* **Adoption:** 80 % of new users complete first analysis in 24 h  
* **Retention:** 30-day active rate ≥ 60 %  
* **Task Success:** FII ≥ 30 %, RFR ≤ 10 %

## Positioning Statement

*"MoveAI gives every athlete a digital coach — instant, accurate, and personalized.*  
 *Film your lift, get feedback in seconds, and see measurable improvement over time."*

## Competitive Landscape & Differentiation

### Market Context

The fitness-tech market has matured around tracking, not teaching.  
Most products quantify activity (reps, calories, steps) but fail to address movement quality — the actual skill behind safe, efficient performance.

| Category | Example Products | Focus | Gap |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Wearables** | Apple Watch, WHOOP, Garmin | Heart rate, strain, recovery | No visual or biomechanical feedback |
| **Tracking / Logging Apps** | Strava, MyFitnessPal, Fitbod | Workout data, nutrition, progress tracking | No technique correction or visual analysis |
| **AI Fitness Content** | Kaia, Tempo, Peloton | Guided workouts with limited AI form feedback | Closed ecosystems; limited personalization |
| **Computer Vision Startups** | Freeletics AI Coach, Kaia Motion Coach | Pose detection for exercise validation | Focused on rep counting or compliance, not mastery |

### MoveAI's Edge

1. **Biomechanical Depth:** MoveAI goes beyond rep counting to full joint-angle deviation tracking, producing measurable form-improvement data (Form Improvement Index).

2. **Real-Time, Personalized Coaching:** Converts pose data into coaching cues and corrective exercises, mirroring what elite trainers actually say.

3. **Safety by Design:** Early-warning detection for risky motion patterns (lumbar rounding, valgus collapse) with red-flag alerts.

4. **Longitudinal Learning:** Tracks trends in movement consistency and offers AI-driven progress insights, not just one-off corrections.

5. **Device-Native, Privacy-First:** On-device inference keeps videos private, reducing latency and compliance barriers for gyms and clinics.

6. **Cross-Domain Potential:** Framework extends beyond powerlifting — adaptable to golf swings, martial arts, and rehabilitation.

## Strategic Position

MoveAI positions itself at the intersection of:

* **AI coaching** (Tempo, Kaia)  
* **Athlete performance analytics** (WHOOP, Coach's Eye)  
* **Injury-prevention tech** (PhysiApp, Hinge Health)

Its unique focus on *movement mastery* creates a durable niche between consumer fitness and professional coaching.

## Strategic Opportunities

### Rehabilitation & Physical Therapy

**Why it fits:**  
MoveAI's pose-estimation and corrective feedback systems are directly applicable to physical therapy and post-injury recovery, where form, range of motion, and progressive improvement are critical.

**Opportunity:**

* Partner with physiotherapists and clinics to create rehab-specific templates (e.g., ACL, rotator cuff).  
* Introduce a "Rehab Mode" where range-of-motion thresholds adapt to patient recovery stages.  
* Integrate with existing PT software for outcome tracking.

**Long-Term Value:**  
Enables recurring revenue from B2B2C licensing to clinics, insurers, or rehab platforms.

### Sport-Specific Coaching

**Why it fits:**  
The same biomechanical analytics powering squat feedback can evaluate golf swings, Muay Thai kicks, or baseball throws. Each vertical has its own market of athletes hungry for measurable improvement.

**Opportunity:**

* Launch "MoveAI for Golf / Tennis / Combat Sports."  
* License models fine-tuned per sport, with visual overlays comparing user motion to pro reference footage.  
* Collaborate with coaches for verified cue libraries ("keep your elbow tucked," "rotate through the hip").

**Long-Term Value:**  
Multi-sport model creates high brand stickiness and cross-sell potential.

### Coach Marketplace

**Why it fits:**  
 Many athletes still crave human validation. MoveAI can empower certified coaches with data-driven insights to accelerate feedback cycles.

**Opportunity:**

* Launch a marketplace connecting users with remote coaches who review AI-analyzed videos.  
* Coaches receive structured data (deviation maps, trend graphs) to personalize feedback.  
* Introduce revenue share or "verified coach" subscription tiers.

**Long-Term Value:**  
Blends scalability of AI with human trust to create a hybrid coaching model.

### Enterprise & Institutional Licensing

**Why it fits:**  
Gyms, universities, and sports academies all need scalable technique monitoring. MoveAI's modular CV engine can integrate via SDK or API.

**Opportunity:**

* Provide white-labeled versions for gym chains or corporate wellness programs.  
* Offer analytics dashboards for coaches, trainers, and athletic departments.  
* Support data privacy standards (HIPAA, GDPR) for institutional clients.

**Long-Term Value:**  
 Expands from consumer SaaS to enterprise contracts — recurring ARR and brand authority.

### Wearables & Sensor Fusion

**Why it fits:**  
 Integrating video with inertial or physiological data deepens context for both safety and performance.

**Opportunity:**

* Integrate Apple Watch / WHOOP data (heart rate, strain) to adjust feedback intensity.  
* Explore real-time barbell or shoe sensors to validate ground contact and velocity.  
* Combine video \+ wearable inputs into a holistic "Performance Health Score."

**Long-Term Value:**  
 Builds technical moat and differentiates MoveAI from pure computer-vision competitors.

### Data Network & Research Insights

**Why it fits:**  
 Aggregated, anonymized movement data offers value far beyond the app — for sports science, ergonomics, and equipment design.

**Opportunity:**

* Publish anonymized benchmarks (average squat depth, back angle stability, asymmetry data).  
* Collaborate with universities or health researchers.  
* Potential for partnerships in biomechanical optimization or injury prediction.

**Long-Term Value:**  
Positions MoveAI as a data authority on human performance and motion health.
